Discover Music on TuneFree

Use TuneFree search to find songs, artists, albums and playlists in one place. Start with a song title, an artist name, a mood, or a genre, then switch tabs to narrow the results.

Category cards help new visitors begin with familiar listening paths such as pop, hip-hop, rock, jazz, classical, lofi and regional trends. Each card opens a focused music search.

Search history stays on your device so repeat searches are faster, while saved favorites and playlists are available when you sign in. The goal is quick discovery without a crowded page.
